## Method

1. Heat oven to 200°C. Lay fish in a small dish with tomato wedges, olives, garlic, thyme, oil.
2. Bake 15–18 minutes. Steam broccoli. Couscous with boiling water.

## Notes

One dish. Almost Provençal. Freeze spare fillets on arrival.
