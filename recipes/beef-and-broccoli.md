## Method

1. Cook rice. Cut broccoli into small florets; slice garlic and ginger.
2. Blanch broccoli 2 minutes in boiling water, drain.
3. Stir-fry mince until browned. Add garlic and ginger, 30 seconds.
4. Add broccoli, oyster sauce, soy, and a splash of water. Toss until glossy.
5. Serve over rice.

## Notes

Mince cooks faster than sliced beef and is easier to portion for one.
