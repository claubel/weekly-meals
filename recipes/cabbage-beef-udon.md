## Method

1. Soak or boil udon per pack instructions. Drain.
2. Stir-fry onion and cabbage until wilted but still crunchy.
3. Add mince and cook through. Season with soy, mirin, chilli, sesame oil.
4. Toss in the udon for 1 minute so the noodles take the sauce.
