## Method

1. Cook rice. Slice ginger and spring onion. Put fish on a plate with ginger.
2. Steam 8–10 minutes until just opaque. Steam pak choi alongside.
3. Pour off watery juices. Top with spring onion. Heat a spoon of oil, pour over, then soy.

## Notes

Cod, basa, or sea bass. Wet-market classic. Freeze extra fillets on shop day.
