## Method

1. Slice chicken, peppers, and onion. Season chicken with cumin, paprika, salt.
2. Pan-fry chicken then veg until charred at the edges. Lime over the top.
3. Warm one wrap. Fill with more veg than wrap, yoghurt instead of sour cream, lettuce.

## Notes

Freeze leftover wraps on shop day. Salad can overflow onto the plate.
