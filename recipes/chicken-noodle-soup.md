## Method

1. Simmer chicken in stock 12 minutes. Lift out, shred.
2. Add sliced carrot and mushrooms to the broth, 5 minutes.
3. Add pak choi and udon (or egg noodles). Return chicken. Soy to taste.

## Notes

Light broth, lots of greens. Use udon from noodle week so you do not buy two noodle types.
