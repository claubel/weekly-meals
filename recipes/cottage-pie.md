## Method

1. Boil potatoes until soft. Mash with a splash of milk, salt, pepper. Keep the lid thin.
2. Brown mince with diced carrot and mushrooms. Add stock, simmer until thick. Stir in peas.
3. Put filling in a small dish, spread mash on top, fork the surface.
4. Bake at 200°C 15–20 minutes until the peaks brown.

## Notes

Batch-friendly. Freeze an extra portion if you doubled the filling.
