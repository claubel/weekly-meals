## Method

1. Heat oven to 190°C. Halve courgette lengthwise, scoop a channel, oil and salt.
2. Brown mince with chopped tomato, garlic if you have it, thyme.
3. Mix in cooked couscous or leftover rice. Pack into the courgette.
4. Bake 20–25 minutes until the courgette is soft.

## Notes

Olive oil, thyme, garlic. Same oven week as stuffed peppers.
