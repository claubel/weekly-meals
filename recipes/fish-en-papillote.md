## Method

1. Heat oven to 200°C. Boil new potatoes on the side.
2. Pile sliced courgette, tomato, fennel, lemon on baking paper. Fish on top. Fold a parcel.
3. Bake 15 minutes. Open at the table.

## Notes

No pan mess. Fennel is optional. Baking paper is the only extra tool.
