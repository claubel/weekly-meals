## Method

1. Boil potatoes. Steam green beans.
2. Pat fish dry, season, dust with flour. Fry in a little oil then butter, 3 minutes per side.
3. Squeeze lemon in the pan with chopped parsley. Spoon over the fish.

## Notes

The French fillet dinner. Cook night one of the potato week.
