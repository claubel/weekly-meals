## Method

1. Dust fish with flour, pan-fry, set aside.
2. Deglaze with stock, lemon, capers. Wilt spinach in the same pan.
3. Couscous or a little rice. Return fish to the sauce.

## Notes

Orzo if you have it; couscous keeps the Mediterranean week to one carb box.
