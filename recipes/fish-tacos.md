## Method

1. Season fish with paprika and salt. Pan-sear, flake.
2. Shred cabbage, dress with lime, yoghurt, coriander. Chop tomato.
3. Two small tortillas, more slaw than wrap.

## Notes

Yoghurt-lime instead of mayo. Cook the fillet within 48 hours of the shop.
