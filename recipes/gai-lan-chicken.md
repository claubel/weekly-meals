## Method

1. Cook rice. Slice chicken thighs into strips. Cut gai lan into lengths.
2. Stir-fry chicken until just cooked. Set aside.
3. Stir-fry garlic and gai lan with a splash of water until stems are tender.
4. Return chicken, add oyster sauce, soy, white pepper. Toss and serve over rice.

## Notes

Thighs stay juicier than breast. Choy sum is a fine swap.
