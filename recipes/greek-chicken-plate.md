## Method

1. Season chicken with oregano, lemon, salt. Grill or pan-cook. Slice.
2. Chop tomato, cucumber, onion, lettuce. Optional feta.
3. Yoghurt mashed with a little garlic and lemon as a dip.
4. One pita, salad as the bulk of the plate.

## Notes

Salad is the meal; pita is the carb. Shares the wraps-week salad bar.
