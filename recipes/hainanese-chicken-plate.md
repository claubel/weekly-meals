## Method

1. Poach chicken in barely simmering water with a slice of ginger, 12–15 minutes. Rest, slice.
2. Cook rice. Blanch pak choi in the poaching liquid.
3. Chop ginger and spring onion, cover with a spoon of hot oil or the poaching fat, add soy.
4. Plate chicken, rice, cucumber, tomato, greens, ginger-scallion oil.

## Notes

Classic HK comfort. If you poach a whole pack on Monday, slice leftover meat into satay salad later.
