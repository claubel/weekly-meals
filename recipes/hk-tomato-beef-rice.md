## Method

1. Cook rice. Slice onion and tomatoes into wedges. Shred lettuce.
2. Brown mince with onion. Add tomatoes, ketchup, soy, and a splash of water.
3. Simmer until tomatoes collapse into a sauce, 8 minutes.
4. Serve over rice with a pile of lettuce on top or on the side.

## Notes

Cha fan energy, but with mince and salad so the plate stays balanced.
