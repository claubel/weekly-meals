## Method

1. Cook a small amount of rice. Wash and dry lettuce leaves. Julienne the carrot.
2. Stir-fry mince with soy, gochujang, and sesame oil until caramelised, 5 minutes.
3. Put a spoon of rice, mince, carrot, and kimchi in each lettuce cup.
4. Eat with your hands. Extra cucumber from the same shop fits here too.

## Notes

Light carb, high veg volume. Gochujang keeps in the fridge for months.
