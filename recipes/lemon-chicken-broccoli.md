## Method

1. Cook rice. Season chicken, pan-sear in oil until cooked, set aside.
2. Steam or fry broccoli in the same pan.
3. Deglaze with stock, lemon juice, and zest. Reduce 1 minute.
4. Return chicken to coat. Serve with rice.

## Notes

A knob of butter in the sauce is optional. Quinoa works instead of rice.
