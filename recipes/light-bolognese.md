## Method

1. Dice carrot, celery, onion, and courgette small.
2. Soften the veg in olive oil 8 minutes. Add mince and brown.
3. Add tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Simmer 15 minutes.
4. Boil spaghetti until al dente. Toss with the sauce (modest pasta portion).
5. Optional: rocket salad on the side if you want more greens.

## Notes

Hide extra veg in the sauce. Freeze leftover sauce if you doubled the mince.
