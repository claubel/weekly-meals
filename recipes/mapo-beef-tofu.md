## Method

1. Cook rice. Cut tofu into cubes; slice pak choi and spring onion.
2. Brown mince in a little oil. Add garlic and chilli bean paste; fry until fragrant.
3. Add a small splash of water or stock and the tofu. Simmer gently 5 minutes so the tofu takes on the sauce. Do not stir hard.
4. Steam or pan-wilt pak choi on the side, 3 minutes.
5. Finish with soy and spring onion. Serve over rice.

## Notes

One pan plus greens. Soft tofu adds protein without another meat purchase.
