## Method

1. Heat oven to 200°C. Chunk courgette, tomato, onion. Toss with oil, oregano, olives, salt.
2. Nestle chicken thighs on the tray. Roast 30 minutes.
3. Pour boiling water over couscous, rest 5 minutes, fluff.
4. Serve chicken and roasted veg over couscous.

## Notes

Almost no stove time. Same olives and tomatoes as tomato-olive chicken.
