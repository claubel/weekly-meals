## Method

1. Mix miso and mirin. Spread on the fillet. Rest 10 minutes while rice cooks.
2. Grill or bake at 220°C 8–10 minutes. Pan asparagus. Slice cucumber.

## Notes

Japanese grocery staples. Broccolini instead of asparagus is fine.
