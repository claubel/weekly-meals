## Method

1. Heat oven to 200°C. Halve new potatoes, chunk carrots, toss with oil and salt on a tray.
2. Mix Dijon with yoghurt. Coat chicken thighs. Sit them on the tray.
3. Roast 25–30 minutes until chicken is cooked through and potatoes are browned.
4. Steam or add green beans to the tray for the last 10 minutes.

## Notes

French default. Crème fraîche works instead of yoghurt.
