## Method

1. Boil potatoes until tender. Boil egg 8 minutes if using. Blanch green beans.
2. Arrange lettuce, tomato, beans, potatoes, olives, drained tuna.
3. Dress with lemon, a little of the tuna oil, salt, pepper.

## Notes

Tinned tuna in olive oil. Proper balanced plate. Egg is optional.
