## Method

1. Cook rice. Steam greens.
2. Warm sardine oil with garlic, onion, chopped tomato until saucy, 6 minutes.
3. Slide sardines in to heat through. Serve over rice with greens.

## Notes

Simple and excellent. Shares tomato and onion with the tin-cupboard week.
