## Method

1. Boil potatoes, mash (or use leftover mash). Cool slightly.
2. Mix with drained mashed sardines, parsley, salt, pepper. Form two cakes.
3. Pan-fry in a film of oil until both sides are crisp, 3 minutes each.
4. Salad on the side.

## Notes

Batch two nights if you boil extra potato. Bind with an egg if the mix is wet.
