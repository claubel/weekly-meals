## Method

1. Boil spaghetti. Warm sardine oil with garlic and chilli.
2. Mash sardines into the oil. Add lemon zest and juice, spinach, parsley.
3. Toss pasta. Modest portion.

## Notes

Sardines melt into the oil. Filling, cheap, omega-3 heavy.
