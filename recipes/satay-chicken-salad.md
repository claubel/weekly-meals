## Method

1. Cook or use leftover rice. Grill or pan-cook chicken, slice.
2. Shred cabbage, cucumber, carrot.
3. Loosen peanut butter with soy, lime, a little water, optional honey. Go easy on the sauce.
4. Toss salad, top with chicken and a small scoop of rice.

## Notes

High crunch. Leftover poached chicken from the Hainanese plate works.
