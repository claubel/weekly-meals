## Method

1. Scrub the potato, prick it, and bake at 200°C for about 25 minutes (or microwave 6–8 minutes, then crisp in a pan).
2. Season the mince with salt and pepper, form one thick patty, and chill 5 minutes so it holds.
3. Boil or steam green beans until just tender, 4–6 minutes. Drain.
4. Pan-sear the patty in a little oil, 3–4 minutes per side, until browned but still juicy.
5. Slice the tomato, dress with olive oil and salt. Serve the steak haché with Dijon on the side.

## Notes

Very French weeknight. Buy 300 g mince if this is paired with another beef night the same week.
