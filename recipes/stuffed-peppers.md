## Method

1. Heat oven to 190°C. Halve peppers, scrape seeds, oil the cut sides.
2. Cook rice if you do not have leftover. Mix with browned mince, diced onion, and chopped tomato. Season.
3. Stuff the peppers, sit in a small baking dish with a splash of water.
4. Bake 25–30 minutes until the peppers are soft.
5. Eat two halves tonight; leftover filling is lunch.

## Notes

Hands-off bake. Same veg as the oven-tray week (peppers, onion, tomato).
