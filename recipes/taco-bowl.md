## Method

1. Cook a small rice base. Season mince with cumin, paprika, salt; brown.
2. Chop tomato, shred lettuce, slice avocado, squeeze lime.
3. Bowl: rice, mince, a lot of salad, yoghurt instead of sour cream.

## Notes

Skip cheese if you want it lighter. Corn tortillas can replace rice.
