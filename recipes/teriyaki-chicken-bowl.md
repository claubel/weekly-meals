## Method

1. Cook rice. Mix soy, mirin, sugar for a quick teriyaki.
2. Pan-cook chicken, glaze with the sauce until sticky.
3. Boil edamame, steam pak choi, slice cucumber.
4. Bowl it: rice, chicken, veg.

## Notes

City Super / Japanese aisle staples. Homemade teriyaki is just soy, mirin, a little sugar.
