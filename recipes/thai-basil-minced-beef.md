## Method

1. Cook rice (or reheat leftover). Slice cucumber into sticks.
2. Smash garlic and chilli. Heat a wok or large pan until very hot.
3. Stir-fry the mince until no longer pink, breaking it up, 3–4 minutes.
4. Add garlic, chilli, fish sauce, and soy. Toss 30 seconds.
5. Kill the heat, fold in a big handful of Thai basil until it wilts.
6. Serve over rice with cucumber on the side.

## Notes

Holy basil if you find it at the wet market; Italian basil is a weaker substitute. ParknShop usually stocks Thai basil.
