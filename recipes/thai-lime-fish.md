## Method

1. Cook rice. Mix fish sauce, lime, chilli, garlic.
2. Steam or pan-cook fish, spoon the dressing over at the end.
3. Stir-fry mixed veg. Herbs on top.

## Notes

Thaw the fillet the morning you cook it if it was frozen on shop day.
