## Method

1. Soften onion and ginger. Add fresh and tinned tomato, stock. Simmer 10 minutes.
2. Drop fillet in, lid on, 6–8 minutes. Wilt spinach. Toast on the side (or leftover rice).

## Notes

HK/French hybrid: ginger + tomato broth. Cook within 48 hours or freeze the fillet.
