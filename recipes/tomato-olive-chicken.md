## Method

1. Brown chicken in olive oil. Add chopped tomato, olives, a splash of water or white wine.
2. Cover and simmer 15 minutes. Steam green beans.
3. Couscous with boiling water, 5 minutes. Serve chicken and sauce over it with beans.

## Notes

Provençal. Pan or oven. Shares olives and tomatoes with the traybake.
