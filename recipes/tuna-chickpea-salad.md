## Method

1. Drain chickpeas and tuna. Chop pepper, cucumber, parsley.
2. Toss with lemon, olive oil, salt. Eat with pita or skip the bread.

## Notes

Chickpeas add fibre. No stove. Keep half the chickpea tin for another salad if needed — or use the whole tin.
