## Method

1. Day-old rice only (or spread fresh rice to dry 20 minutes).
2. Scramble egg, set aside. Stir-fry frozen veg.
3. Add rice, tuna, soy. Keep oil modest. Finish with egg and spring onion.

## Notes

Best leftover-rice dinner after a steamed-fish night. Use 150 g cooked rice (about 70 g dry cooked earlier).
