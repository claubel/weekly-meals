## Method

1. Cook rice. Mix drained tuna with a little mayo and soy.
2. Bowl over rice with cucumber and lettuce.
3. Instant miso with cubed tofu and optional wakame on the side.

## Notes

Tuna + a little mayo + soy. Miso soup is the veg/protein boost.
