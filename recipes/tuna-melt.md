## Method

1. Mix tuna with mustard. Pile on one slice of sourdough. Few slivers of cheese.
2. Grill until the cheese melts.
3. Big salad of lettuce and tomato is half the plate — cheese is a topping, not the meal.

## Notes

Open-faced. Freeze the rest of the loaf on shop day.
