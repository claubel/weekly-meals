## Method

1. Use leftover rice or microwave a portion. Thaw edamame in hot water.
2. Mix tuna with soy, sesame, rice vinegar.
3. Bowl: rice, tuna, cucumber, avocado, edamame, torn nori.

## Notes

No cooking if rice is leftover. Eat the avocado mid-week.
