## Method

1. Boil spaghetti. Soften garlic and chilli in a little tuna oil.
2. Add tinned tomatoes, capers, olives, chopped fresh tomato. Simmer 5 minutes.
3. Fold in tuna and spinach off the heat. Toss pasta. Modest portion.

## Notes

Pantry meal. Side salad if you want more greens.
