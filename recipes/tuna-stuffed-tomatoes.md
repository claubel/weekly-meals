## Method

1. Slice tops off two large tomatoes, scoop flesh, chop it.
2. Mix tuna, mustard, herbs, a little couscous or leftover rice, chopped tomato flesh.
3. Fill, eat cold or bake 10 minutes at 180°C. Cucumber salad on the side.

## Notes

Mix tuna, herbs, mustard, olive oil. Good Friday tin meal.
