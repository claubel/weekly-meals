## Method

1. Soften garlic in olive oil. Add tomatoes and drained beans. Simmer 8 minutes.
2. Wilt spinach. Stir tuna in at the end so it stays in chunks.
3. Toast to dunk.

## Notes

Tuscan-ish pantry stew. Friday meal in the tin-cupboard week.
